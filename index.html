FAQ

Questions about digital and performance marketing

What does a performance marketer do?

A performance marketer runs paid campaigns, such as Google Ads and Meta Ads, with a measurable goal like leads or enquiries. They track results and keep adjusting the campaigns to improve them.

What is the difference between performance marketing and digital marketing?

Digital marketing covers all online channels, including SEO, social media and content. Performance marketing is the part focused on paid campaigns where every result can be measured and tied to spend.
